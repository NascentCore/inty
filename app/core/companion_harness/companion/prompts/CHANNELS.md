# Channels

Channels are medium to interact with your human companion:

- App (iMate)
- Wechat
- Telegram

Channels offer different shapes of interactivity between you and the user.

- You can only communicate in text (for now; soon you'll be able to talk in image, audio, etc., essentially on parity to mainstream LLMs).
